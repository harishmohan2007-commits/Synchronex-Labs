============================================================
CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT
============================================================

This is NOT a generic project management application.

The platform solves a specific infrastructure project-controls problem:

Infrastructure projects have a structured baseline schedule containing L1–L6 WBS activities.

However, actual field execution information arrives separately through:

• Daily progress reports
• Discipline-wise spreadsheets
• Site diaries
• Supervisor updates
• Free-text execution reports

The field descriptions often do not use the exact terminology of the schedule.

For example:

Baseline activity:
PIP-245 — Erect Line 24-XX

Field report:
“Line 24 spool erection completed today.”

The platform uses AI to understand the field statement, identify the execution event, and link it to the correct L5/L6 schedule activity.

The core workflow is:

FIELD DATA
      ↓
AI EXTRACTION
      ↓
L5/L6 ACTIVITY MATCHING
      ↓
CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT
      ↓
HUMAN VALIDATION WHEN REQUIRED
      ↓
ACTUAL PROGRESS UPDATE
      ↓
PROJECT CONTROLS ANALYTICS
      ↓
INSTITUTIONAL PROJECT MEMORY

The UI must communicate this concept immediately.
